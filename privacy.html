Relay – Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Relay is a video player for iPad. It plays no content of its own: you sign in with your own IPTV service, and Relay connects directly to that service. This policy explains what happens to your information.

We don't collect your data

Relay has no accounts, servers, analytics, advertising, or tracking. We don't collect, receive, store, sell, or share any personal information, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

What stays on your iPad

To work, Relay saves the following only on your device:

  • Your IPTV service sign-in details, stored in the iPad's secure Keychain.
  • Your favorites, watch history and resume positions, and your audio and subtitle preferences.
  • Your settings, and a cached copy of your service's TV guide.

This information never leaves your iPad except as described below. You can remove it by signing out in Settings (sign-in details), using "Clear watch history" in Settings, or deleting the app (everything).

Connections the app makes

  • Your IPTV service. Relay connects directly to the server you enter to load channels, the guide, movies, shows, images, and video. That service's own privacy policy applies to those connections.
  • Trailers and links. If you tap a trailer or a link, it opens in YouTube or your browser, and their privacy policies apply.

Children

Relay is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
